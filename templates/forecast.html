Model Assumptions, Data Requirements & Limitations
Methodological Governance
Underlying Assumptions:
  • Linearity: The relationship between predictors and sales is assumed to be linear.
  • Temporal Stationarity: Assumes historical coefficients remain stable across future intervals.
  • Residual Normality: Prediction bounds rely on homoscedastic, normally distributed residuals.
Known Limitations:
  • No Extrapolation Guarantee: Inputs significantly higher or lower than historical ranges carry heightened uncertainty.
  • External Shocks: Unmeasured market events (macroeconomics, competitor moves) cannot be predicted by this model.
  • Non-Causality: OLS captures historical statistical correlation rather than controlled causality.
